# Commitment Rules

Most people who start a DSA problem list stop partway, often more than once. That's not a character flaw. Plans that run on motivation fail because motivation always runs out. These rules run on **habits and small wins** instead.

---

## Rule 1: The minimum day
On a bad day, the **minimum** counts as a full day:
> **1 review + 15 minutes on anything** (a new problem, flashcards, re-typing a template).

Most days you'll do more once you start. But the promise you make to yourself is only the minimum, and you can always keep that promise.

## Rule 2: Never miss twice
Missing one day is life. Missing two days is the start of quitting. If you missed yesterday, today is a **minimum day at least**, no matter what.

## Rule 3: Same time, same place
Attach practice to something you already do every day ("after morning coffee," "right after work, before phone"). Write your slot here:
> My practice slot: `at 7 pm after triage meeting`

## Rule 4: One list, no restarts
Don't restart from problem 1 when you come back after a break. **Continue where you stopped** and let the review system bring back what you forgot. Restarting feels productive, but it's why people never reach the Advanced patterns.

## Rule 5: Make progress visible
- Tick the checkbox in the pattern note when you solve a problem.
- Fill in the 2-minute daily log ("Open today's daily note" creates it from the template); the streak is in the daily notes.
- Look at the [[Dashboard]] counts. Watching the numbers go up is motivating.

## Rule 6: Quality over count
One problem **understood and re-solved** beats five problems watched. Don't chase numbers.

## Rule 7: Weekly check-in
Every week, spend 15 min on a weekly review (new note in `05 - Weekly/`, insert the `Weekly Review` template): what worked, what didn't, and one change for next week.
